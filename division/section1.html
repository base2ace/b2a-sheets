Quick Divisibility Rules Guide (2, 3, 4, 5)

÷ 2

The last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, or 8).

÷ 3

The sum of all digits is divisible by 3.

÷ 4

The number formed by the last 2 digits is divisible by 4, or the number ends in 00.

÷ 5

The last digit is either 0 or 5.
